---

Simplified file-tile icon for ".euo" source files of the Eunoia language,
where source code is a poem.

A slightly rounded square tile (squircle) in deep indigo (#2B2D42) with a thin
cream border. Inside: a folded-corner ivory paper sheet that is blank except
for three short handwritten-style poetry lines stacked like verses, the middle
line ending in a small amber star mark. A tiny fine-tipped black quill rests
across the bottom-right corner of the sheet, its nib leaving a delicate ink
swirl that merges into the star.

Flat, minimal, high-contrast, crisp edges — must stay readable at 16px and
recognizable at 128px. Subtle flat drop shadow outside the tile. Background:
transparent or the cream paper tone; no extra text.
